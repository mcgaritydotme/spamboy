I'm still thinking about [Hawaiian Interstate Highway signs](/hawaiian-interstate-highways).  And many, many more!

After reading about [their interstate highways](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_state_highways_in_Hawaii), I fell down the Wikipedia rabbit hole and found myself on the [numbered highway signs article](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Numbered_highways_in_the_United_States).  Then spent way too much with the table below.

I've ranked the highways signs from best-to-least, using the following criteria:

- Overall aesthetics, including shape & color
- Quality of the typography
- Line & border weights
- Any iconography they featured
- Ability to communicate where you are
