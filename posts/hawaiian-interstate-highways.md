While in Hawaii, I took notice of all the unique highway signage that otherwise wouldn't be found back in Texas.

I was particularly curious about their interstate highways, all of which begin with "H" (as opposed to an "I").

Then my brain melted as I learned they aren't numbered using the same logic as domestic routes: They're numbered in the order they were built.  H-1, followed by H-2 then H-3.  So naturally H-201 comes next.  

And even/odd numbering does not represent their orientation.  H-1 and H-3 go east/west, while H-2 runs north/south.

Very pupule!
