I've found great joy lately working on my hobby site [Presidents In Time](https://presidentsintime.com).  Besides its built-in history lesson, I get immense satisfaction from the various acts of creation: curating the photos, validating the references, balancing the portrayals so they illustrate the march of each man's life, and the wonder of seeing them presented side-by-side in a large grid.

I sometimes wish there weren't so many presidents, tho.  It's taking time to complete.  I wanted to have it done in time for 4th of July, but I'm only halfway there.
